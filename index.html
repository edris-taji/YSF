About Us

We support activism for democracy in Afghanistan

For the last 6 years, we at The Independence Project have been helping human rights activists, opposition coalitions and members of the civil society with vital resources to bring about a true democratic transition in Afghanistan. As a privately funded program, our outreach has so far been limited but powerful.

  • Promote true Democracy
  • Prevent Corruption
  • Ensure Human Rights

About Founder

Sayed Habibullah Hossaini was born in 1994 Herat, Afghanistan. He grew up in a middle-class family. His parents were both educators and instilled a love of learning in their children from a young age. Mr. Hossaini excelled in his studies and obtained a Bachelor's degree in law and political science from Jami University. Activities

Youth Savior Foundation Activities

Educational

Holding educational class, short-term seminars and educational capacity building programs for girls and boys.

Sports

Creating and launching sports programs for people and adolescents for growth in the community.

Cultural

Setting up and holding seminars to inform about the culture and traditions of different ethnic groups.

Social

Participating in popular activities such as cleaning the city, organizing litigation programs for the people.

Health

Setting up first aid classes for men and women and awareness seminars for the general public.
